    FAQ

    Good to know

    What's the difference between "Quick match" and "Recruiter score"?

    Quick match compares the meaning of your resume and the job post using AI embeddings. It's instant, and good for ranking many jobs. The Recruiter score is a deeper review: the AI grades 6 areas recruiters and ATS software check, and the app combines them with fixed weights into a score out of 100.

    How is the Recruiter score calculated?

    Skills & keywords 30%, relevant experience & projects 25%, measurable impact 15%, education 10%, complete sections 10%, and clarity 10%. Facts like contact info, sections and numbers in your bullets are measured by code, not guessed by the AI.

    Is my resume saved?

    No. Your file is read to get the text and then discarded. Only the job posts you add are saved, and only you can see them in this browser.

    Why does it sometimes take a while?

    It runs on Google Gemini's free tier. When it's busy the app automatically switches to another model. Results are saved, so opening the same job again is instant.
